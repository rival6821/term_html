Privacy Policy

Browser Cache Cleaner  ·  Effective date: 5 May 2026

Summary

No data collected No data transmitted No third-party sharing No analytics Works locally only

What this extension does

Browser Cache Cleaner is a Chrome extension that allows you to clear your browser's cached files with a single click. The extension uses Chrome's browsingData permission solely to remove locally cached files on your device when you click the button.

Data we do not collect

Browser Cache Cleaner does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or other user data. This includes, but is not limited to:

Browsing history Cookies Location data Authentication credentials Financial information Health information Communications Website content

How the extension works

All processing occurs entirely on your device. When you click Clear browser cache, the extension sends a local message from the popup to the background service worker, which then calls chrome.browsingData.removeCache. No data leaves your device at any point.

The extension does not use remote code execution, third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, or any form of tracking technology.

Permissions

The only permission requested is browsingData, which is used exclusively to clear the browser cache when you initiate the action. No other permissions are declared or used.

Changes to this policy

If future versions of Browser Cache Cleaner introduce any feature that involves collecting or transmitting user data, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are released.
